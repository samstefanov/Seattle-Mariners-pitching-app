## Purpose

- An MLB article was published which ranked the best rotations in the MLB going into 2024. The Mariners, a little surprisingly to me, were ranked number 1 over teams like the Braves and Dodgers. The five starting pitchers in the rotation all played at some point last year so the question is, "How good were the Mariners pitching rotation in 2023?" and "How good will they be in 2024?". Their pitching rotation consists of Luis Castillo, George Kirby, Logan Gilbert, Bryan Woo and Bryce Miller.

## Data
- This application has data from the baseballr package. Baseballr is a package written for R
focused on baseball analytics and has data collected from other baseball databses such as FanGraphs,
Baseball-Reference, and baseballsavant.
